The idea behind the challenge

What if we stopped asking
and started building?

The Black Buck Challenge begins with a simple symbol: one dollar. It is not the limit of the vision. It is a declaration of participation — “I am Black. Count me in.”

Small individual acts can become a visible expression of shared purpose when people organize around a clear goal.
